## 📖 Core drill (6 min)

### Organelles and functions

| Organelle | Function |
|---|---|
| **Cell wall** | Supports and protects the cell; prevents bursting |
| **Cell membrane** | Controls what enters and leaves the cell (selectively permeable) |
| **Cytoplasm** | Where most chemical reactions of the cell take place |
| **Nucleus** | Contains the genetic material / controls cell activities |
| **Mitochondrion** | Site of **aerobic respiration** — releases energy |
| **Chloroplast** | Site of **photosynthesis**; contains chlorophyll |
| **Vacuole** | Contains cell sap; provides support |
| **Ribosome** | Site of **protein synthesis** |
| **Plasmid** | Small circular piece of DNA (bacteria) |

### Which cell has what

| | Plant | Animal | Fungal | Bacterial |
|---|:--:|:--:|:--:|:--:|
| Cell wall | ✅ **cellulose** | ❌ | ✅ **different material** | ✅ **different material** |
| Cell membrane | ✅ | ✅ | ✅ | ✅ |
| Cytoplasm | ✅ | ✅ | ✅ | ✅ |
| Nucleus | ✅ | ✅ | ✅ | ❌ |
| Mitochondria | ✅ | ✅ | ✅ | ❌ |
| Chloroplasts | ✅ | ❌ | ❌ | ❌ |
| Vacuole | ✅ (large, permanent) | ❌ | ✅ | ❌ |
| Ribosomes | ✅ | ✅ | ✅ | ✅ |
| Plasmid | ❌ | ❌ | ❌ | ✅ |

**The three facts that get examined:**
1. **Plant** cell walls are made of **cellulose**; **fungal** and **bacterial** cell walls are made of **different materials**. (You do **not** need to know which — that's explicitly excluded.)
2. **Fungal cells** are like plant and animal cells but have a **different cell wall** and **no chloroplasts**.
3. **Bacterial cells** have **no membrane-bound organelles** (no nucleus, no mitochondria, no chloroplasts) and a **different cell wall**; they have a **plasmid**.

## ✍️ Exam reps (8 min)

1. Name the organelle that is the site of aerobic respiration. (1)
2. State **two** structures found in a plant cell but **not** in an animal cell. (2)
3. A cell has a cell wall, a nucleus, mitochondria, ribosomes and a vacuole, but no chloroplasts. Is it plant, animal, fungal or bacterial? Explain. (2)
4. State **two** ways a bacterial cell differs from a plant cell. (2)
5. Name the structure in a bacterial cell that consists of a small circle of DNA. (1)

<details>
<summary>▶ Show answers</summary>

1. **Mitochondrion**. **(1)**
2. Any two: **chloroplast**, **cell wall**, (large permanent) **vacuole**. **(2)**
3. **Fungal** (1). It has a **cell wall and a vacuole** like a plant cell, but **no chloroplasts**, so it cannot photosynthesise (1). **(2)**
4. Any two: bacterial cell has **no nucleus**; **no mitochondria**; **no chloroplasts**; has a **plasmid**; its cell wall is made of a **different material** (not cellulose). **(2)**
5. **Plasmid**. **(1)**

**Marking note:** for Q4, "bacteria are smaller" is not a structural difference from the spec list. Stick to the organelles.

</details>

## 🏆 Boss question (3 min)

Yeast is a single-celled fungus used to make bread. Onion epidermis cells are plant cells.

**Give one structural similarity and two structural differences between a yeast cell and an onion epidermis cell. (3)**

<details>
<summary>▶ Show boss answer</summary>

**Similarity (any one):** both have a **cell wall** / a **nucleus** / **mitochondria** / a **vacuole** / **ribosomes** / a **cell membrane**. (1)

**Differences (any two):**
- The onion cell wall is made of **cellulose**; the yeast cell wall is made of a **different material** (1)
- The onion cell has **chloroplasts**; the yeast cell does not (1)

*Note: onion epidermis cells are often drawn without chloroplasts in practicals because they grow underground — if a question shows you a diagram, answer from the diagram, not from the general rule.*

</details>
