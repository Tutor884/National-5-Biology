## 📖 Core drill (6 min)

### Pesticides and accumulation
- Pesticides **sprayed onto crops** can **accumulate in the bodies of organisms over time**
- As they are **passed along food chains**, **toxicity increases**
- It can reach **lethal levels** — usually in the **top consumer**, which has eaten the most contaminated prey

> 🧠 **Hook:** the animal at the **top** of the chain gets the **worst** dose, because it eats many contaminated organisms and the pesticide is **not broken down or excreted**.

### Two alternatives to pesticides

**Biological control** — using a **natural predator (or parasite)** of the pest to reduce its numbers.
- ✅ No toxic chemical to accumulate in food chains; doesn't leach into water
- ⚠️ Risk: the control organism may itself become a pest, or eat non-target species

**GM crops** — crops **genetically modified**, e.g. to be **resistant to pests**, reducing the need for pesticide; or to use nitrogen more efficiently, **reducing the need for fertiliser**.

### The quick comparison table

| | Fertilisers | Pesticides | Biological control | GM crops |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Purpose | Increase yield via nitrates | Kill organisms reducing yield | Reduce pest numbers naturally | Reduce need for fertiliser/pesticide |
| Main risk | **Leaching → algal bloom** | **Accumulation → toxicity up the food chain** | Control species becomes a pest | (Discussion point — no fixed N5 fact required) |

## 🏆 Boss question (3 min)

A pesticide was banned after populations of a bird of prey crashed. The pesticide was found at 0.02 ppm in lake water, 0.5 ppm in small fish, 2 ppm in large fish and 25 ppm in the birds.

a) Calculate how many times greater the concentration in the birds is than in the lake water. (1)
b) Explain the pattern in the figures. (2)

<details>
<summary>▶ Show boss answer</summary>

a) 25 ÷ 0.02 = **1250 times**. **(1)**
b) The pesticide is **not broken down / not excreted**, so it **accumulates in the bodies** of organisms (1). Each organism eats **many** contaminated organisms from the level below, so the concentration **increases at each stage of the food chain** (1). **(2)**

*Calculation tip: for "how many times greater", divide big ÷ small. Write the working — the mark is for the answer but working saves you if you slip.*

</details>
