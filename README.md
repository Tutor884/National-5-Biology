## How this works

One mission per day. **20 minutes. 17:00. No excuses.**

Each mission file has four parts:

| Part | Time | What it is |
|---|---|---|
| ⚡ **Warm-up** | 3 min | Rapid-fire recall from yesterday. No notes. |
| 📖 **Core drill** | 6 min | The facts for today's key area, written the way SQA wants them |
| ✍️ **Exam reps** | 8 min | Real exam-style questions. Write answers out. |
| 🏆 **Boss question** | 3 min | One nasty application question for the A grade |

Answers are hidden behind a `▶ Show answers` dropdown. **Write your answer first, then open it.** Peeking early is just reading, and reading is the least effective revision method there is.

## Scoring

- Mission completed → **10 XP**
- Boss question right → **+5 XP**
- Full streak day → **+2 XP**

Log it in [`SCOREBOARD.md`](SCOREBOARD.md). 200 XP by 5 October = ready.

## Rules of the game

1. **Write, don't read.** Every answer goes on paper or screen before you look.
2. **Mark yourself honestly.** A mark you gave yourself that SQA wouldn't give is a mark you lose in the test.
3. **Red flags get a re-run.** Anything you got wrong goes in the "Repeat Offenders" list at the bottom of the scoreboard and comes back on Day 13.
4. **Missed a day?** Don't double up. Just carry on — the plan is front-loaded on purpose.
