## 2. The five sequences (8 min) — write these from memory, cold

These are the ones that carry 3 and 4 marks each. If you can produce all five, you can pass this test on those alone.

1. **Photosynthesis** — the two stages, and what passes between them
2. **The algal bloom chain** — from leaching to dead fish, all seven steps
3. **Natural selection** — the four steps
4. **Speciation** — isolation to two species
5. **Enzyme denaturation** — why the rate falls

<details>
<summary>▶ Check yourself</summary>

1. **Light reactions:** light trapped by chlorophyll in chloroplasts → chemical energy → **ATP**; water split into **hydrogen** and **oxygen**; oxygen diffuses out. → passes **hydrogen and ATP** → **Carbon fixation:** enzyme-controlled; hydrogen + ATP + **CO₂** → **sugar**.
2. Leaching → **algae increase / bloom** → **light reduced** → **aquatic plants die** → **bacteria feed on dead algae and plants** → **bacteria increase greatly** → **oxygen used up** → fish die.
3. More offspring than the environment can sustain → **variation** and a **selection pressure** → **best adapted survive and reproduce**, passing on **favourable alleles** → those **alleles increase in frequency**.
4. **Isolation barrier** (geographical / ecological / behavioural) → **different mutations** in each sub-population → **natural selection selects differently** because of different selection pressures → so **genetically different** they are **two species**.
5. Enzyme is **denatured** → the **shape of the active site changes** → **no longer complementary to the substrate** → rate of reaction falls.

</details>
