## 📖 Core drill (6 min)

### Why we need increased yield
The **human population is increasing**, so **food yield must increase**. Two main tools: **fertilisers** and **pesticides**.

- **Fertilisers** provide chemicals such as **nitrates**, which **increase crop yield**.
- **Pesticides** kill plants and animals that **reduce crop yield**.

### The nitrate story
1. **Nitrates dissolved in soil water** are **absorbed into plants**
2. Plants use nitrates to make **amino acids**
3. Amino acids are **synthesised into plant proteins**
4. **Animals eat plants (or other animals)** to obtain **amino acids** for their own **protein synthesis**
5. **Fertiliser is added to soil to increase its nitrate content**

> 🧠 **Hook:** nitrate → amino acid → protein. Three words, in that order. Miss out "amino acids" and you drop a mark.

### The algal bloom chain — learn it in this exact order
1. Fertiliser **leaches** into fresh water, adding **extra unwanted nitrates**
2. **Algal populations increase** → an **algal bloom**
3. The bloom **reduces light levels**, so **aquatic plants die**
4. **Dead plants and dead algae** become **food for bacteria**
5. **Bacteria increase greatly in number**
6. Bacteria **use up large quantities of oxygen**
7. **Oxygen availability is reduced** for other organisms (e.g. fish die)

**Reducing the problem:** **GM crops** can be used to **reduce the use of fertilisers**.

> ⚠️ **Do not** get dragged into the full nitrogen cycle — it is explicitly **not required** at N5.

## ✍️ Exam reps (8 min)

1. Explain why fertilisers increase crop yield. (2)
2. Describe how nitrates absorbed by a plant are used. (2)
3. Describe how the leaching of fertiliser into a loch can lead to the death of fish. (4)
4. State one way that GM crops can reduce the environmental impact of farming. (1)

<details>
<summary>▶ Show answers</summary>

1. Fertilisers provide **nitrates** (1), which plants use to make **amino acids / proteins**, increasing growth and therefore yield (1). **(2)**
2. Nitrates are used to produce **amino acids** (1), which are **synthesised into plant proteins** (1). **(2)**
3. Four marks from this chain — you need the sequence, not just the ending:
   - Nitrates leach into the water, **algal populations increase / algal bloom** forms (1)
   - The bloom **reduces light**, so **aquatic plants die** (1)
   - **Bacteria feed on the dead algae and plants and increase greatly in number** (1)
   - Bacteria **use up large quantities of oxygen**, so there is **not enough oxygen** for the fish (1) **(4)**
4. GM crops can **reduce the use of fertilisers** (or reduce the use of pesticides). **(1)**

**Marking note:** Q3 — the single most common error is jumping from "algal bloom" straight to "fish die". The **bacteria + oxygen** step is where two of the four marks live.

</details>

## 🏆 Boss question (3 min)

A farmer applies fertiliser to a field in heavy rain in October, when the crop has already been harvested.

**Explain why this is both wasteful for the farmer and damaging to a nearby river. (3)**

<details>
<summary>▶ Show boss answer</summary>

- With **no crop growing**, there are **no plant roots to absorb the nitrates** from the soil water (1).
- Heavy rain means the nitrates are **leached** out of the soil into the river rather than being used — so the fertiliser is wasted (1).
- The extra nitrates in the river cause an **increase in algal populations / algal bloom**, which sets off the light → plant death → bacteria → oxygen depletion chain (1). **(3)**

</details>
