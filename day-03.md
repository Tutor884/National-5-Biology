## 📖 Core drill (6 min)

Photosynthesis is a **two-stage** process.

### Stage 1 — Light reactions
- **Light energy** from the sun is **trapped by chlorophyll** in the **chloroplasts**
- Light energy is converted into **chemical energy**, which is used to **generate ATP**
- **Water is split** into **hydrogen** and **oxygen**
- **Oxygen diffuses out of the cell** (by-product)
- **Passes on to stage 2:** hydrogen + ATP

### Stage 2 — Carbon fixation
- A series of **enzyme-controlled** reactions
- Uses **hydrogen** and **ATP** (from the light reactions) plus **carbon dioxide**
- Produces **sugar (glucose)**

> 🧠 **Hook:** Light reactions *split water, make ATP*. Carbon fixation *fixes CO₂ into sugar*. The two things handed between them are **hydrogen and ATP** — that exact pair is a mark.

### What happens to the sugar
- Used in **respiration** (releases the chemical energy), **or**
- Converted into other substances:
  - **Starch** → **storage**
  - **Cellulose** → **structural** (cell walls)

### Limiting factors
Only **three**: **carbon dioxide concentration**, **light intensity**, **temperature**.

A limiting factor is the factor **in shortest supply** that holds back the rate of photosynthesis.

**Reading a limiting-factor graph:**
- **Rising line** → the factor on the x-axis **is** limiting the rate
- **Plateau (flat line)** → that factor is no longer limiting; **something else** is (one of the other two)
- **Temperature curves that fall after a peak** → enzymes are being **denatured** (carbon fixation is enzyme-controlled)

## 🏆 Boss question (3 min)

A grower keeps a greenhouse at 25 °C with bright lighting. Adding a CO₂ generator increases tomato yield. The following summer, with the same lighting and CO₂ generator running, the grower notices the yield increase is smaller on very hot days when the greenhouse reaches 42 °C.

**Explain this observation. (3)**

<details>
<summary>▶ Show boss answer</summary>

- At 25 °C, CO₂ concentration was the **limiting factor**, so adding CO₂ increased the rate of photosynthesis and yield (1).
- At 42 °C the **enzymes controlling carbon fixation are denatured** (1) — their **active site changes shape**, so the rate of photosynthesis falls (1).
- Therefore extra CO₂ can no longer increase the rate, because **temperature** is now limiting. **(3)**

*A-grade move: name which factor is limiting **at each stage**, not just "temperature is too high".*

</details>
