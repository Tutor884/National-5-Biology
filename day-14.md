    b) State **three** ways the remaining energy was lost. (3)

16. Potato discs were placed in sucrose solutions. Change in mass was: 0.0 M → +9 %; 0.3 M → +2 %; 0.5 M → −4 %; 0.7 M → −11 %.

    b) Explain the result at 0.7 M. (2)

17. An enzyme's activity was measured at pH 2, 4, 6, 8 and 10. Activity peaked at pH 4.
    a) Explain the low activity at pH 10. (2)
    b) Describe how the pupil could find the optimum pH more accurately. (1)
    c) The experiment was carried out once at each pH. State how reliability could be improved. (1)
