## 📖 Core drill (7 min)

### Speciation — the five steps

1. Part of a population becomes **isolated by an isolation barrier**
2. The barrier can be **geographical**, **ecological** or **behavioural**
3. **Different mutations** occur in each sub-population
4. **Natural selection selects for different mutations** in each group, because of **different selection pressures**
5. Each sub-population **evolves** until they are so **genetically different** that they are **two different species**

> 🧠 **Hook:** **I·M·S·S** — Isolation, Mutation, Selection, Speciation.

### The three barriers

| Barrier | What it means | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **Geographical** | A physical barrier separates them | Mountain range, river, sea, glacier |
| **Ecological** | Different conditions/habitats within the same area | Different **pH**, **salinity**, or different habitats |
| **Behavioural** | Differences in behaviour stop them breeding | Different courtship displays, different mating songs or times |

**Scottish examples worth naming:** **Arran whitebeam**, **St Kilda wren**, **Arctic charr**.
