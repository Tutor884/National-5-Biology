## UNIT 3 — LIFE ON EARTH

### 1 Ecosystems
- **Population** = one species · **Community** = all organisms · **Ecosystem** = community + non-living components it interacts with
- **Niche** = the **role** an organism plays: resources it needs, interactions with others, conditions it tolerates
- **Competition** happens when resources are in **short supply**
- **Interspecific** = different species, one or a few resources · **Intraspecific** = same species, **all** resources, **more intense**

### 2 Distribution of organisms
- **Biotic** (living): competition, disease, food availability, grazing, predation
- **Abiotic** (non-living): light intensity, moisture, pH, temperature
- Errors → fix with: **don't shade the meter · clean the probe · let it settle · repeat and average**
- **Quadrats** (plants), **pitfall traps** (invertebrates). Improve: **more**, **random**, **same size**, **covered trap**
- **Indicator species** = **presence or absence** indicates environmental quality / pollution level
- **Paired-statement keys** — be able to use and construct

### 3 Photosynthesis
- **Light reactions:** light trapped by **chlorophyll** in **chloroplasts** → chemical energy → **ATP**; **water split** into **hydrogen + oxygen**; oxygen diffuses out
- **Carbon fixation:** **enzyme-controlled**; **hydrogen + ATP + CO₂** → **sugar**
- Sugar → **respiration**, or **starch (storage)** / **cellulose (structural)**
- **Limiting factors:** CO₂ concentration, light intensity, temperature
- Plateau on a graph = **something else** is now limiting. Fall after peak = **enzymes denatured**

### 4 Energy in ecosystems
- Energy lost as **heat, movement, undigested materials**; only a very small quantity goes to **growth**
- **Pyramid of numbers** = counts, can be **irregular** (one big producer; parasites)
- **Pyramid of energy** = **kJ/m²/year**, **always a true pyramid** (energy always lost)

### 5 Food production
- Rising population → more yield → **fertilisers** (nitrates) and **pesticides**
- **Nitrate → amino acids → plant proteins**; animals eat plants to get **amino acids** for protein synthesis
- **Algal bloom chain:** leaching → **algae increase** → **light reduced, plants die** → **bacteria increase greatly** → **oxygen used up**
- **Pesticides accumulate** in bodies; **toxicity increases along the food chain** to **lethal levels**
- Alternatives: **biological control** (natural predator) and **GM crops** (reduce fertiliser/pesticide use)

### 6 Evolution of species
- **Mutation** = **random change to genetic material**; neutral / advantageous / disadvantageous; **spontaneous**; **only source of new alleles**; **radiation and some chemicals** raise the rate
- **Variation** allows a population to evolve in response to a changing environment
- **Natural selection:** more offspring than can be sustained → **selection pressure** → **best adapted survive and reproduce** → **favourable alleles increase in frequency**
- **Speciation:** **isolation barrier** (**geographical / ecological / behavioural**) → **different mutations** in each sub-population → **natural selection selects differently** → **two species**

## UNIT 1 — CELL BIOLOGY (1.1 – 1.4)

### 1 Cell structure
| Organelle | Function |
|---|---|
| Cell wall | Support and protection |
| Cell membrane | Controls entry/exit; selectively permeable |
| Cytoplasm | Site of chemical reactions |
| Nucleus | Genetic material; controls the cell |
| Mitochondrion | **Aerobic respiration** |
| Chloroplast | **Photosynthesis** |
| Vacuole | Cell sap; support |
| Ribosome | **Protein synthesis** |
| Plasmid | Small circle of DNA (bacteria) |

- Plant wall = **cellulose**; fungal and bacterial walls = **different materials**
- **Bacteria:** no nucleus, no mitochondria, no chloroplasts; **have a plasmid**
- **Fungi:** like plant/animal cells but **different cell wall** and **no chloroplasts**

### 2 Transport across cell membranes
- Membrane = **phospholipids and proteins**, **selectively permeable**
- **Passive** = down the gradient, **no energy**: **diffusion** (molecules, high → low) and **osmosis**
- **Osmosis** = movement of **water molecules** from **higher water concentration to lower water concentration** through a **selectively permeable membrane**
- **Active transport** = **requires energy**, uses **membrane proteins**, moves substances **against** the gradient
- Animal cell: **bursts** (in water) / **shrinks** (in concentrated solution)
- Plant cell: **turgid** / **plasmolysed** — the **cell wall** prevents bursting

### 3 DNA and the production of proteins
- **Double-stranded helix**, **complementary base pairs**: **A–T**, **C–G**
- **Base sequence determines amino acid sequence**
- **Gene** = **section of DNA that codes for a protein**
- **mRNA** carries a **complementary copy** of the code **from DNA in the nucleus to a ribosome**, where the protein is **assembled from amino acids**
- Not required: uracil; details of transcription/translation

### 4 Proteins
- Variety of shapes and functions arises from the **sequence of amino acids**
- Functions: **structural, enzymes, hormones, antibodies, receptors**
- **Enzymes** = **biological catalysts**, made by **all living cells**, **speed up** reactions, **unchanged**
- **Active site complementary to specific substrate**; **enzyme–substrate complex** forms → **product(s)**
- **Degradation** (starch → maltose, by amylase) and **synthesis** (glucose-1-phosphate → starch, by phosphorylase)
- **Optimum** conditions; affected by **temperature and pH**; **denatured** = **change in shape** → rate falls

## 🚫 The ten answers that score zero

1. "Niche is where an organism lives" → that is a **habitat**
2. "The enzyme dies / is killed" → **denatured**
3. "Energy is used up" → energy is **lost as heat, movement, undigested materials**
4. "The bacteria adapted to the antibiotic" → the **mutation was already there**; the antibiotic **selected** it
5. "Osmosis is movement from high to low concentration" → must say **water** concentration and **selectively permeable membrane**
6. "Algal bloom kills the fish" → the **bacteria using up oxygen** kill the fish
7. "A gene is part of DNA" → must say it **codes for a protein**
8. "Be more careful next time" → **repeat and average**
9. "Light reactions make energy" → they **generate ATP**
10. A calculation with **no unit** → no mark
